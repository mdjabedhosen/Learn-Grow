Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 9, 2025

At Learn & Grow ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website learnandgrow.io and use our e-learning platform services.

By accessing or using our services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information when you use our platform:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, and password
  • Profile Information: Profile picture, bio, educational background, learning preferences
  • Payment Information: Billing address, payment method details (processed securely through third-party payment gateways - we do not store complete card details)
  • Identity Verification: For instructor accounts, we may require additional verification documents

1.2 Learning Data

To provide personalized learning experiences, we collect:

  • Course Enrollment: Courses you enroll in, purchase history, and enrollment dates
  • Progress Tracking: Lessons completed, quiz scores, assignment submissions, time spent on lessons
  • Certificates: Courses completed and certificates earned
  • Interaction Data: Comments, forum posts, questions asked, peer interactions

1.3 Technical Information

We automatically collect certain technical information when you use our platform:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, clickstream data, referring websites
  • Cookies and Tracking: See our Cookie Policy for detailed information
  • Log Files: Access times, error logs, system activity

1.4 Communications

  • Emails you send us
  • Support tickets and chat messages
  • Survey responses and feedback
  • Newsletter subscriptions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

2.1 Provide and Improve Services

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process course enrollments and payments
  • Deliver course content and learning materials
  • Track your progress and issue certificates
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Improve our platform, courses, and user experience

2.2 Personalization

  • Recommend courses based on your interests and learning history
  • Personalize your dashboard and learning path
  • Adapt content difficulty to your skill level

2.3 Communication

  • Send transactional emails (enrollment confirmations, password resets, receipts)
  • Notify you about course updates, new lessons, and announcements
  • Send marketing emails about new courses and promotions (with your consent)
  • Respond to your support requests

2.4 Analytics and Research

  • Analyze platform usage and user behavior
  • Monitor course effectiveness and completion rates
  • Conduct research to improve educational outcomes
  • Generate anonymized statistical data

2.5 Security and Legal

  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect our rights and property

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

3.1 With Your Consent

We will share your information when you explicitly consent, such as when you choose to share your profile or achievements on social media.

3.2 Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our platform:

  • Payment Processors: bKash, SSLCommerz, Nagad for processing payments
  • Cloud Hosting: Vercel, MongoDB Atlas for hosting and database services
  • Email Services: Resend, SendGrid for sending transactional and marketing emails
  • Analytics: Google Analytics for understanding platform usage
  • Content Delivery: Cloudinary for image and video hosting
  • Video Platform: YouTube, Vimeo for hosting course videos

3.3 Instructors

If you enroll in a course, we share necessary information with the course instructor, including your name, progress, and quiz scores, to facilitate your learning experience.

3.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government regulation, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.

3.5 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS
  • Password Protection: Passwords are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt
  • Access Controls: Limited employee access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct periodic security assessments
  • Secure Infrastructure: Our servers and databases are hosted on secure, monitored platforms

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax records for 7 years)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements
  • Maintain course completion records and certificates indefinitely

When you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

6.1 Access and Correction

You can access and update your account information at any time by logging into your account settings.

6.2 Data Portability

You can request a copy of your data in machine-readable format by contacting us at privacy@learnandgrow.io

6.3 Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time. Note that some information may be retained as required by law.

6.4 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of marketing emails by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email or updating your preferences in account settings.

6.5 Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for details.

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at privacy@learnandgrow.io and we will delete it.

For students aged 13-17, we recommend parental guidance when using our platform.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in Bangladesh or other countries where our service providers operate. By using our services, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your residence.

9. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., payment gateways, video platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting a notice on our website
  • Sending an email to your registered email address
  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy

Your continued use of our services after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us:

Learn & Grow

Email: privacy@learnandgrow.io

Support: support@learnandgrow.io

Website: learnandgrow.io

Response Time: We aim to respond within 48 hours

Your Privacy Matters: We are committed to transparency and protecting your rights. If you have any concerns about how we handle your data, please don't hesitate to reach out.
